# Testing Sabicars as a buyer — the brief for trainees

*For the Inova trainees. Read it once before you start. Record yourself as before.*

You are not reviewing a website. You are a person with money who wants a car and does not want to lose that money. Your job is to find what would stop you, confuse you, or make you distrust Sabicars. **Finding nothing wrong means you did not look hard enough.** On your Jiji review, neither of you noticed that anyone can list anything there, that prices can be bait, and that nobody checks the cars. Look for that kind of thing here.

Use the live site: **https://sabicars.vercel.app** (or sabicars.com after the switch). Use your phone for at least half of it.

## Your tasks — do them, don't describe them

1. **Find a Toyota Highlander you could get with ₦5 million down**, and book a viewing for it. Write down every step, and every word you did not understand.
2. **Try to find out if one car is genuine.** Pick any car. Find out: Is the price real? Has anyone inspected it? Can you see what was found? Where is the chassis number? Could you check the company is registered? Say what convinced you and what did not.
3. **Try to get scammed.** Act like a careless buyer. Is there any way to pay, reserve or commit without seeing the car? Does any page promise something it cannot prove? Does anything look like a trick, a hidden fee, or a "pay to join"?
4. **Ask a question at a strange hour** (after 9pm). Use the form on a car's page, and Ask Sabicars. Did you get a reference number? Did anyone reply, and when? Did the reply know which car you meant?
5. **Try to earn.** Find out how you would make money from Sabicars without buying a car. How long until you understood it? Would you trust it? Did anything feel like a get-rich scheme?
6. **Get lost on purpose.** Open More. Open Help. Try every button you see. Report any button or link that does nothing, or lands on a page that does not match its name.
7. **Compare with Jiji** on three things only: how fast you understood the site, how sure you were the car was real, and what happened when you asked a question.

## What to record

- Each task: done / could not do / gave up — and where.
- Every word you did not understand, exactly as written.
- Every moment you felt stressed, suspicious or lost, and what was on the screen.
- Three things Sabicars does better than Jiji, and three things Jiji does better.
- One sentence: would you send your mother here to buy a car?

## What we do with it

Every confusing word, every dead button and every missing proof you find becomes a change on the site, and the next test checks that it is gone. Your findings from October 2026 already changed the names of every page.
